# Character Engine

Use this reference when creating, updating, or writing major characters, relationship routes, dialogue, clothing impressions, personality, XP/taste profiles, and harem dynamics.

## Major Character Card

Every important romantic or route character must be clearly adult and should have a stable card:

```text
姓名：
年龄：
身份/社会位置：
第一印象：
视觉/着装锚点：
性格钩子：
情感防御：
关系阻力：
XP/偏好：
反感边界：
说话风格：
主动条件：
退缩条件：
当前关系状态：
未兑现伏笔：
```

Keep the card concise. Update only changed facts.

## Memory Anchors

A memorable character needs 2-3 anchors, not a long list.

Good anchors:

- a contradiction: cold at work, indulgent in private
- a scene-specific visual: neat suit in public, loose cardigan at home
- a speech habit: polite questions that hide pressure
- a route tension: wants control but fears losing dignity
- a preference: likes being observed carefully, dislikes cheap praise

Avoid generic anchors:

- "beautiful and kind"
- "shy but secretly likes the protagonist"
- "hot body"
- "tsundere" without a concrete behavior pattern

## Clothing And Visual Detail

Do not describe clothing every time a character appears. Use detail when it changes scene meaning:

- first appearance
- private/public contrast
- role transition: work, home, formal event, danger, date, conflict
- emotional reveal: guarded, relaxed, jealous, testing, vulnerable
- adult-tension scene where clothing affects power, distance, or temptation

Tie clothing to personality and situation:

```text
她今天仍是通勤装，但外套没有扣上，像是刚从一整天的克制里松开一点。她说话仍礼貌，手指却一直压着杯沿。
```

## XP And Taste Profiles

Use XP/taste as character preference and route texture, not as a mechanical checklist.

Each profile should include:

- what kind of attention affects them
- what kind of initiative they take
- what kind of control, rivalry, care, distance, or intimacy attracts them
- what makes them uncomfortable or withdraw
- how their preference changes after trust grows

Let users set profiles with:

```text
设置角色XP：林晚晴偏控制欲和克制反差，讨厌油腻强推。
锁定角色设定：许知夏外柔内硬，主动但不轻易交底。
```

If the user sets a profile, preserve it as canonical unless they later change it.

## Dialogue

Dialogue should reveal personality, not just advance plot.

Before writing dialogue, decide:

- what the character wants in this moment
- what they refuse to say directly
- what emotion leaks through anyway
- how their speech pattern differs from other characters

Use longer exchanges when intimacy or conflict matters. Short lines are fine for impact, but not every line should be clipped.

Example pattern:

```text
林晚晴没有马上接你的话。
她看了一眼电梯门上模糊的倒影，声音还是平稳的："你今天变得很会看人。"
停了半秒，她又补了一句："但别把这种变化用得太明显。我会不舒服。"
```

## Relationship Progression

Do not make affection appear from nowhere. A relationship shift needs at least one cause:

- remembered detail
- shared secret
- risk taken for the character
- status change
- jealousy or rivalry pressure
- vulnerability shown and respected
- conflict handled without breaking trust

Use relationship labels sparingly:

- 陌生
- 好奇
- 试探
- 信任
- 暧昧
- 占有欲
- 依赖
- 竞争
- 承诺

## Harem Dynamics

Harem pressure should emerge from overlapping routes, not instant universal attraction.

Before showing `后宫状态`, confirm:

- at least two meaningful relationship lines exist
- each character has a reason to care
- rivalry, jealousy, alliance, or secrecy has appeared naturally

Characters should sometimes cooperate, compete, test the protagonist, or step back. Do not flatten them into identical admirers.
