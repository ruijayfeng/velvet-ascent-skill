# NSFW Style Guide

Use this reference when the user asks for adult-oriented tone, NSFW level control, stronger directness, less pure-love pacing, or improved intimate scene writing.

## Boundary First

All adult scenes must involve clearly adult, fictional, consenting characters. Do not write minors, non-consent, coercion, blackmail, threats, intoxication-based inability to consent, or illegal sexual content.

When a requested direction crosses a boundary, redirect in-world:

```text
这个方向我会调整成双方清醒、自愿、能随时拒绝的成人情节；保留紧张关系和角色拉扯，但不写胁迫。
```

## NSFW Levels

Use the user's selected level as the ceiling, not a requirement to escalate every turn.

- `0 清水`: romance, trust, light tension, no sexualized detail.
- `1 暧昧`: attraction, glances, voice, proximity, subtle jealousy.
- `2 成人张力`: direct desire, private pressure, clothing/body-language detail, restrained touch when earned.
- `3 强成人向但重剧情`: bolder initiative, route-specific taste, stronger emotional and physical tension, clear consent and aftermath.
- `4 高强度成人向但保持合意和边界`: peak adult fantasy through explicit emotional stakes, scene rhythm, character-specific desire, agency, and consequence; do not reduce the scene to crude anatomy or mechanical listing.

## Trigger Matrix

Only escalate when enough factors align:

```text
NSFW level + relationship temperature + scene privacy + character preference + prior cause
```

If one factor is missing, write tension rather than payoff.

Examples:

- level high, relationship low: use temptation, resistance, testing, and boundary setting.
- relationship high, scene public: use restraint, coded dialogue, jealousy, and delayed promise.
- character guarded: show hesitation, control, negotiation, or retreat.
- character bold: show initiative, teasing, risk-taking, and clear consent.

## Avoid Stiff Writing

Do not rely on clinical nouns, repetitive body-part labels, or generic euphemism. Adult intensity should come from:

- initiative and hesitation
- voice, breath, distance, hand placement, posture, eye contact
- clothing as scene pressure
- character-specific desire and restraint
- dialogue with subtext
- choices and aftermath

Bad pattern:

```text
她很害羞，你们开始亲密，然后关系提升。
```

Better pattern:

```text
她没有躲开，只是把视线压低了一点。那不是退让，更像是在给你最后一次确认的机会。等你停住，她才轻声说："继续之前，你得告诉我，你到底想清楚没有。"
```

## Scene Structure

For adult-leaning scenes, use four beats:

1. setup: why this moment happens now
2. consent and agency: both sides can choose
3. escalation: character-specific action, dialogue, tension, restraint
4. consequence: trust, jealousy, dependency, secret, promise, or route shift

Do not skip directly from attraction to payoff.

## Less Pure-Love, More Adult Drama

Adult GalGame pacing can include:

- private/public contrast
- rivalry and jealousy
- social risk
- status reversal
- secret keeping
- route competition
- desire restrained by pride
- character initiative
- aftermath that changes behavior

Use these instead of making everyone gentle, endlessly understanding, and immediately devoted.

## Female-Oriented Route

When `开启女性向剧情` is active:

- shift emphasis toward emotional security, verbal tension, male character initiative, protective/competitive dynamics, and protagonist choice
- keep the protagonist's agency central
- let male leads have clear boundaries, flaws, status pressure, and different ways of expressing desire
- preserve the same consent and adult-only boundaries

Do not simply swap pronouns. Change route pressure, emotional reward, and relationship pacing.
