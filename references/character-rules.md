# Character And Dialogue Rules - 角色和对话规则

Every important character must have a complete card, including the 6-dim differentiation matrix entry, the fetish profile (3-5 items, each with a specific trigger → body reaction → tell), and the fetish interaction library.

## Character Card Template

Each important character must include:

### Basic Information
- 姓名
- 年龄
- 职业
- 外貌

### Personality
- 性格特征
- 说话习惯

### Fetish Profile

- 每项包含：触发条件 → 身体反应 → 表现特征

### Fetish Interaction Library
- 性癖互动库

### Desire Stance

### Visual/Clothing Anchors

### Resistance Points
- 阻力点

### 6-Dim Differentiation Matrix
- 性癖组合
- 性互动人格
- 欲望立场
- 场景偏好
- 触发器
- 关系阻力源

No two characters may share ≥3 dimensions of similarity.

## Dialogue Guidelines

Dialogue should reveal personality and desire, not just advance plot. Before writing dialogue, decide:

- what the character wants in this moment
- what she refuses to say directly
- what desire leaks through body language, voice, distance, hand placement, eye contact
- how her speech pattern differs from other characters

Use longer exchanges when intimacy or conflict matters. Short lines are fine for impact, but not every line should be clipped.

Do not make every character instantly love the protagonist. Attraction grows through specific events, remembered details, mutual risk, status change, rivalry, jealousy, and private choices.

## Character Differentiation

No two characters may share ≥3 dimensions of similarity. This ensures each character is unique and memorable.

The 6 dimensions are:
1. 性癖组合
2. 性互动人格
3. 欲望立场
4. 场景偏好
5. 触发器
6. 关系阻力源

See `references/character-engine.md` for the full differentiation matrix specification.
